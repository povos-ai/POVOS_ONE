# POVOS ONE Constitution

> **Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Status:** Draft  
> **Document Type:** Foundational Architecture Constitution

---

# 1. Vision

Build the world's most extensible, intelligent, secure, and configurable Enterprise Platform Operating System.

POVOS ONE is designed to power governments, enterprises, startups, NGOs, educational institutions, political organizations, and future digital ecosystems from a single platform.

---

# 2. Mission

**Build Once. Extend Forever.**

The Platform Kernel should remain stable while unlimited business capabilities can be added through reusable capabilities, modules, extensions, and marketplace packages.

---

# 3. Core Philosophy

POVOS ONE is not a single application.

POVOS ONE is a Platform.

Applications are built on the Platform.

Capabilities power the Platform.

Modules compose Capabilities.

Extensions connect external systems.

AI enhances every layer.

---

# 4. The Seven Constitutional Laws

## Law 1 — Everything is Metadata

Nothing important should be hardcoded.

Menus, dashboards, workflows, reports, permissions, forms, and AI behavior should be metadata-driven whenever practical.

---

## Law 2 — Everything is Configurable

Organizations should configure the platform without modifying source code whenever possible.

---

## Law 3 — Everything is Extensible

Every capability should support future extension.

---

## Law 4 — Event Driven Architecture

Modules communicate through events rather than tight coupling.

---

## Law 5 — AI Native

Artificial Intelligence is a core platform capability.

It is not an optional add-on.

---

## Law 6 — Multi-Tenant by Design

The platform must support multiple independent organizations using the same installation while keeping their data isolated.

---

## Law 7 — Backward Compatibility

Stable APIs and platform contracts should evolve carefully to reduce breaking changes.

---
