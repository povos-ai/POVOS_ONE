# Platform Definition

POVOS ONE is a Universal Enterprise Platform Operating System.

It is not a business application.

It is not a government portal.

It is not a CRM.

It is not an ERP.

It is a platform capable of building all of them.

---

# Vision Statement

Create one extensible platform capable of supporting unlimited organizations, unlimited industries, unlimited modules and unlimited future digital ecosystems.
